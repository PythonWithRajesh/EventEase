FAQ & TRANSPARENCY

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our portal, venue verification, and booking guarantees.

What is Amazing Ahmedabad Celebration Portal? +
Amazing Ahmedabad is Ahmedabad's official dedicated celebration platform. We connect citizens and global visitors to verified Navratri passes, hourly open celebration party lawns, grand 5-star wedding banquet halls, luxury hotel staycations, and authentic Amdavadi street foods — all with zero brokerage and instant confirmation.
Are the venues and photos physically verified? +
Yes, 100%! Every banquet hall, party plot, and hotel room is physically visited and verified by our Ahmedabad team. We photograph the actual lawn condition, AC capacity, bridal dressing rooms, and parking space to ensure total accuracy.
Are there any extra broker or middleman charges? +
Zero! We do not charge any broker fees or commission from our customers. The price you see is direct from the venue or event organizers.
How do I receive my booking pass and invoice? +
Immediately upon successful payment, your digital pass with a unique QR code is displayed on your screen, accessible anytime from your account dashboard, and downloadable as an official GST-compliant PDF receipt.
Can I contact the developer or concierge team directly? +
Absolutely! You can message us directly on WhatsApp or submit an inquiry through our Contact page. Our local team responds promptly to assist with custom wedding arrangements or bulk event bookings.
