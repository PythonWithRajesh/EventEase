Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about venue hiring, online payments, and pass downloads.

How does hourly party plot and banquet hall hire work?
Unlike traditional booking agents that mandate paying for a full 24-hour day, Amazing Ahmedabad lets you rent party plots for 1–6 hours and wedding banquet halls for 2–12 hours. You pick your exact start time and duration, and our system calculates the exact hourly total automatically.
Are double bookings or time slot overlaps prevented?
Yes, 100%! When you choose a date and time slot for a lawn or hall, our backend checks all confirmed reservations in real time. If another party has reserved that plot between 6:00 PM and 10:00 PM, nobody else can book that overlapping window.
What payment methods do you accept?
We support both Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Visa/Mastercard/RuPay Credit and Debit Cards, Net Banking) and Cash on Delivery / Pay at Venue. For cash reservations, your booking is confirmed immediately and shows the balance due at check-in.
How do I access and download my PDF ticket or booking receipt?
After confirming your reservation, visit My bookings in your account menu. Every confirmed booking provides full event/venue details and a one-click Download PDF Receipt button.
Can I cancel or manage my reservation?
Yes! You can view and cancel any active booking from your My bookings portal prior to the event start time or hotel check-in.
